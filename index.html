Your 6 months start from the day your account is unlocked.

    6. Using the AI. The AI tutor, essay feedback, evidence and AI-made tests are there to help you learn. Do not hand in AI-written work as your own at school. The AI can make mistakes, so check important facts, and always check evidence with its source before you quote it.

    7. Behaviour. Be respectful. Usernames, feedback messages and anything you type into the site must not be rude, offensive, bullying or inappropriate. This includes tests you share publicly: everyone with an account can see and take a public test, along with your name unless you choose to hide it. The site owner can remove any public test. Accounts that break this rule may be suspended without a refund. Your username and points can be seen by other users on the leaderboard. If you hide your points in Settings, your name is hidden too and you show as "Hidden player".

    9. Safety and privacy. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to these terms before you sign up. We store your email, username, progress, points, your saved writing, notes, flashcards and calendar, and your AI tutor chats so the site can work. The AI can see what's coming up in your calendar so it can help you prepare (you can turn this off on the calendar). The AI can also remember short notes about you from your chats (like topics you find tricky or a test coming up) so it can help you better next time; it is told not to remember private details, and you can see, delete or turn off these memories in Settings. Only you and the site owner (admin) can see your saved writing, notes, calendar, AI chats and AI memories. We never sell your data. You can ask for your account and data to be deleted at any time using Feedback.

    Privacy Policy

    1. What we collect. Your email, display name, password (stored securely by our login provider, Supabase — we never see it in plain text), your practice progress and points, saved writing, notes, flashcards, calendar events, any tests you build or save, and your AI tutor chat history and AI memories (short notes the AI keeps about topics you find tricky, if you turn this on).

    2. Why we collect it. To run your account, track your progress, show you a leaderboard (if you choose to appear on it), let the AI tutor help you and remember useful context, and let you come back to your saved work from any device.

    3. Who can see it. Only you and the site owner (admin) can see your saved writing, notes, calendar, AI chats and AI memories. Your username and points can be seen by other users on the leaderboard, unless you hide this in Settings. If you publish a test, other users can see its questions and your name, unless you hide your points.

    4. Where it's stored. Your data is stored with Supabase, our database and login provider, who process it on our behalf. When you use the AI tutor, AI test-writing or AI marking features, the text you send (your questions, answers or writing) is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic's Claude) to generate a response — it is not used to train their models. We never sell your data to anyone.

    5. Children's privacy. This site is used by school students. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian should agree to this Privacy Policy and the Terms & Conditions before you sign up. If you are under 13, a parent or guardian must agree on your behalf — there's a checkbox for this when you create an account. Parents or guardians can contact us via Feedback (once logged in) to review, change or delete their child's data at any time.

    6. How long we keep it. We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your account, your progress, saved writing, notes, calendar, flashcards and AI chat history are deleted straight away, and your profile is anonymised. A record that an account once existed may be kept briefly for admin purposes (like unlocking payments), with your personal details removed.

    7. Your rights. You can see and delete your own AI memories at any time in Settings. You can delete your own notes, calendar events and saved writing from their pages. You can delete your whole account and data at any time from Settings → Help & legal → Delete my account. You can also ask us anything about your data via Feedback.

    8. Security. We rely on Supabase's security (encrypted connections, hashed passwords, access rules so only you and the admin can reach your private data). No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

    9. Changes to this policy. If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll let you know the same way we tell you about Terms & Conditions changes.

    10. Contact. Questions about your privacy or data can be sent via Feedback, or to the site owner, Jayden Narayan, directly.
